## 🌟 Upgraded Experience Modules

### 01 — The First 5 Seconds & Cinematic Entry
- **Pure Black Opening**: Starts in total darkness with no navigation, no cards, and no interface clutter.
- **Faint Sound Visualization**: A delicate gold frequency line ripples into the center.
- **Shifting Coordinates**: Coordinates materialize and morph smoothly across sanctuaries:
  `35.0116° N // 135.7681° E` (Kyoto) → `51.1784° N // 115.5708° W` (Banff).
- **AERIS Typography**: Appears in high-contrast editorial serif, followed by *"Go somewhere. Feel everything."*
- **Atmospheric Reveal**: The background photograph emerges from darkness as a narrow portal and expands smoothly to fill the entire viewport.
- **Scroll to Enter**: Seamlessly hands over to user interaction, separating typographic planes with layered depth.
- **Accessibility**: Instant fade-in provided if `prefers-reduced-motion` is detected.

### 02 — Custom Editorial Cursor (Desktop)
- Smooth linear interpolation (lerp) physics.
- Minimalist gold dot accompanied by a magnetic circular ring.
- Contextual morphing:
  - Expands to **`VIEW`** over photographs and visual media.
  - Expands to **`EXPLORE`** over destination titles.
  - Soft scale transition over interactive controls.
  - Automatically hidden on touch and mobile devices.

### 03 — Hero Depth Parallax & Opening Sanctuary Selector
- Multi-layer visual depth: Foreground typographic planes, mid-plane atmospheric haze, and background high-res photography.
- Subtle mouse depth parallax without wobble.
- Interactive sanctuary selector allowing switching the hero destination directly (**Kyoto**, **Banff**, **Petra**, **Zermatt**, **Amalfi**) with instantaneous telemetry recalibration.

### 04 — Destination Chapters (Mini-Film Sequence)
- Avoids generic card grids. Each sanctuary is staged as an expansive editorial chapter:
  1. **Massive Heroic Chapter Screen**: Full-bleed opening photo with monumental serif typography.
  2. **Extreme Whitespace & Monocle Stark Prose**: Philosophical essence quote and evocative narrative.
  3. **Architectural Diptych Grid**: Photographic media paired with a live condition matrix (local time with ticking seconds, seasonal conditions, adventure index, daily benchmark) and landmarks list.

### 05 — Dark World Atlas (Scientific Cartography)
- Deep obsidian ocean backdrop with geographic graticules and continents.
- Pulsing coordinate beacon markers across 9 global sanctuaries.
- Physical camera glide across continents when selecting markers.
- Telemetry glass dossier displaying live conditions and quick story navigation.

### 06 — Procedural Audio Atmospheres ("Listen to the Place")
- Real-time Web Audio API synthesis without external audio files:
  - 🌊 *Ocean Waves* (Amalfi)
  - 🌧️ *Rainforest Mist* (Bali)
  - 🏔️ *Alpine Wind* (Zermatt)
  - 🔔 *Zen Singing Bowls* (Kyoto)
- HTML5 Canvas audio waveform visualizer and tactile volume slider.
- Visual atmosphere color grade shifts subtly when audio activates. 100% opt-in (no autoplay).

### 07 — Tactile 3D Postcard Studio
- Physical fine stationery simulation with paper grain, ink postmarks, and realistic 3D card flip.
- Choose destination scenes, affix collectible postal stamps (🌸, 🏔️, 🏛️, 🌌), and compose handwritten correspondence.
- Integrated Print / Save functionality.

### 08 — Fullscreen Photographic Lightbox
- Minimalist luxury photographic experience (`01 / 05 | KYOTO, JAPAN | [Esc] Close`).
- Keyboard navigation (`←` / `→` arrow keys, `Esc` dismiss).

### 09 — Fullscreen Photographic Vibe Matcher
- Pure photographic decision panels with high-fashion typography.
- Hover depth zoom and coordinate reveal.
- Seamless multi-step decision flow leading to a cinematic full-screen destination recommendation.

### 10 — My Expedition (Luxury Travel Dossier)
- Styled as a curated travel itinerary route:
  `KYOTO — 04 DAYS` ↓ `BANFF — 05 DAYS` ↓ `ZERMATT — 05 DAYS`.
- Dynamic investment calculation with smooth numerical tweening (travelers, duration, hospitality tier).
- One-click formatted itinerary clipboard export.

### 11 — 1-Second Atmospheric Theme Crossfade
- Instantaneous full-environment transitions across:
  - 🌌 **Cosmic Midnight** (Obsidian & muted gold)
  - 🌅 **Golden Hour** (Warm sepia & terracotta)
  - 🌿 **Emerald Sanctuary** (Lush pine & dew mist)
